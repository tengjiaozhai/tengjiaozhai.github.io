## From single-step revision to verifiable improvement loops从单步修订到可验证的改进循环

Recent advances in agentic systems allow AI systems to conduct experiments, interpret feedback, and modify their own workflows, transforming Recursive Self-Improvement from a theoretical concept into an engineering problem. Yet the field still lacks a unified account of what constitutes RSI and how its improvement loop should be evaluated.  
近年来，智能体系统的进展使得AI系统能够进行实验、 解读反馈并修改自身的工作流程，将递归自我改进从理论概念转变为工程问题。 然而，该领域仍缺乏对RSI构成要素及其改进循环应如何评估的统一认识。

This survey defines RSI boundaries, distinguishes four stages (evolution, self-evolution, meta-evolution, and recursive self-improvement) and anchors recursion in state inheritance: an accepted improvement from one cycle becomes the starting state of a later cycle, while “recursive progress” is reserved for the stronger claim that the inherited state also improves the improver. It introduces a common Proposal → Feedback → Optimization loop, organizes methods across four evolution targets, and identifies research gaps around evidence-based acceptance, reuse across cycles, and transfer beyond the tasks or evaluators that produced the gains.  
本调查界定了RSI的边界，区分了四个阶段（进化、 自我进化、元进化以及递归自我改进），并将递归锚定在状态继承上： 一个周期中被接受的改进成为后续周期的起始状态，而 “递归进展”则保留给更强的论断，即继承的状态也改进了改进者本身。 它引入了一个共同的 提案 → 反馈 → 优化循环，将方法组织在 四个进化目标上，并识别出围绕循证接受、 跨周期复用，以及超越产生收益的任务或评估者的迁移方面的研究空白。

## A common loop for modern RSI systems现代RSI系统的共同循环

RSI is anchored in state inheritance: an improvement accepted in one cycle is retained as the starting state of a later cycle. Recursive progress (the stronger claim that the inherited state also improves the improver) is treated separately.  
RSI以状态继承为基础：在一个循环中被接受的改进会保留为后续循环的起始状态。递归进展（更强的论断，即继承的状态也能改进改进者本身）则被单独处理。

**Proposal 提案**

Select an editable target and generate a candidate change through prompting, search, evolution, program synthesis, training, or experimentation.  
选择可编辑的目标，并通过提示、搜索、进化、程序合成、训练或实验来生成候选修改。

- Target selection identifies what the loop is allowed to change.  
	目标选择确定循环允许更改的内容。
- Candidate generation creates concrete artifacts such as prompts, tools, memory, code, data, or checkpoints.  
	候选生成创建具体的工件，如提示、工具、记忆、代码、数据或检查点。

**Feedback 反馈**

Expose consequences in a repository, simulator, benchmark, training run, or live environment, then convert outcomes into evidence.  
在代码库、模拟器、基准测试、训练运行或实时环境中暴露后果，然后将结果转化为证据。

- Execution environments reveal behavioral effects of a candidate.  
	执行环境揭示候选方案的行为影响。
- Verification signals judge the evidence through tests, rewards, model judges, formal checks, or review.  
	验证信号通过测试、奖励、模型评审、形式化检查或人工审查来评判证据。

**Optimization 优化**

Retain, revise, reject, promote, or roll back the candidate while preserving memory, provenance, versioning, and validation evidence.  
保留、修订、拒绝、推广或回滚候选方案，同时保留记忆、来源、版本控制和验证证据。

- Update policies decide whether an apparent gain should persist.  
	更新策略决定了一个明显的收益是否应持久化。
- Memory and lineage determine whether later cycles can reuse or audit the change.  
	记忆和谱系决定了后续周期能否重用或审计该变更。

## What changes, how it is tested, and what persists哪些发生了变化，如何对其进行测试，以及哪些内容得以保留

Across the systems we survey, the same anatomy recurs: a target is edited, the edit is exposed to an environment that produces evidence, and a decision layer retains or discards it. Six components organize these concerns into three stages: what can be modified, how it is tested, and what persists.  
在我们所调查的各类系统中，相同的结构反复出现：一个目标被 编辑，编辑后的结果暴露于产生证据的环境中，然后由决策层决定保留或丢弃。六个组件将这些要点组织为三个阶段：可以修改什么、如何测试、以及什么得以留存。

**Proposal 提案**

Choose what to change, then generate a candidate through prompting, search, evolution, synthesis, or training.  
选择要修改的内容，然后通过提示、搜索、演化、合成或训练生成候选方案。

**Feedback 反馈**

Run the change and turn its consequences into evidence via reference, rubric, and judge.  
执行修改，并通过参考、评分标准和评审将结果转化为证据。

**Optimization 优化**

Decide what persists and what is saved, tracked, and reused across cycles.  
决定什么得以保留，以及跨循环保存、跟踪和复用什么内容。

## An evidence-oriented evaluation profile以证据为导向的评估体系

The seven properties below form a single evidence-oriented evaluation profile rather than seven independent capabilities. The organizing test is acceptance, reuse, and transfer: an accepted change must be credible, usable in later cycles, and helpful beyond the evaluator that admitted it.  
以下七个属性构成一个以证据为导向的整体评估框架，而非七项独立的性能指标。其核心检验标准是接受、复用和迁移：一项被接受的变更必须可信、可在后续循环中使用，并且能超越最初采纳它的评估者发挥作用。

**Reliability 可靠性** Does the change improve the intended capability, not just the measured score? 该改动是否提升了预期能力，而不仅仅是提升了测量分数？

False positives can be written into memory, skills, or release pipelines; evaluations should report exploit resistance, calibration, trajectory integrity, and held-out transfer.  
误报可能被写入记忆、技能或发布流程中；评估应报告利用抵抗能力、校准情况、轨迹完整性以及保留数据上的迁移表现。

**Efficiency 效率** Can the loop improve within finite compute, time, and review budgets? 循环能否在有限的计算、时间和审查预算内实现改进？

RSI needs cost frontiers: gains should be measured against compute, wall-clock time, failed attempts, deployment cost, and review burden.  
RSI需要成本边界：收益应针对计算量、墙钟时间、失败尝试次数、部署成本和审查负担来衡量。

**Diversity 多样性** Do proposals cover meaningfully different hypotheses and execution paths? 提案是否涵盖了有实质差异的假设和执行路径？

Diversity is about causal independence, not just different wording; systems should preserve disagreement, branch distance, and failure coverage.  
多样性关乎因果独立性，而非仅仅是措辞不同；系统应保留分歧、分支距离和失败覆盖范围。

**Persistence 持久性** Does a successful intervention remain available and useful later? 一项成功的干预措施，在后续阶段是否仍然可用且有效？

Retained artifacts need provenance, scope conditions, retirement criteria, and evidence that they contribute downstream rather than drifting into noise.  
保留的产物需要具备来源追溯、适用范围条件、淘汰标准，以及能够证明其在后续过程中有所贡献而非逐渐沦为噪音的证据。

**Adaptability 适应性** Can the system respond to changing tasks, tools, evaluators, and environments? 系统能否应对不断变化的任务、工具、评估者和环境？

The key split is frozen evidence for comparison versus adaptive evidence for discovering new weaknesses as the environment changes.  
关键的分歧在于：冻结证据用于对比，而自适应证据则用于在环境变化时发现新的弱点。

**Controllability 可控性** Are authority, review, monitoring, and rollback coupled to risk? 权威、审查、监控和回滚是否与风险挂钩？

Update authority should track target scope, verifier strength, deployment context, and failure severity, with intervention points preserved across the loop.  
更新权限应跟踪目标范围、验证器强度、部署上下文和故障严重性，并在循环中保留干预点。

**Generalization 泛化** Does the improvement transfer beyond its original task and evaluator? 改进是否超越了其原始任务和评估器实现迁移？

The strongest claim is transfer of the improvement procedure itself: later generations should search, evaluate, or optimize better because of what was retained.  
最有力的主张是改进过程本身的迁移：后代应当因为所保留的内容而更擅长搜索、评估或优化。

Recursive self-improvement has moved from theory to engineering practice; the remaining challenge is to make its gains credible, reusable, and transferable beyond the tasks and evaluators that produced them.  
递归自我改进已从理论走向工程实践； 剩下的挑战在于，如何使其收益在催生这些收益的任务和评估者之外，变得可信、可复用且可迁移。
